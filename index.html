Recent papers

  • Juggling Intuitions about Causation and Omissions

    In Advances in Experimental Philosophy of Causation, ed. Wiegmann and Willemsen, 2022

    I discuss the interplay between intuitions of different types in our theorizing about causation.

  • Responsibility and Causation

    In Oxford Handbook of Moral Responsibility, ed. Nelkin and Pereboom, 2022

    I discuss the role that causation plays in both derivative and basic responsibility.

  • The Concept of Responsibility in the Ethics of Self-Defense and War

    Philosophical Studies, symposium on responsibility and defensive harm, 2021

    I discuss “Responsibility Views” in the ethics of self-defense and war, and the concept of responsibility at work in them.
